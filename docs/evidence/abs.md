# Evidence log: abdominal ("six-pack") training

Gathered Nov 15 2026, before any decision. Source: Europe PMC searches (titles/abstracts) on abdominal
exercise and fat, spot reduction, rectus abdominis EMG, abdominal hypertrophy, and core training in
runners. ABS = abstract read; FT = full text read.

## Does training the abs reveal them?

### A1. Vispute SS, et al. J Strength Cond Res 2011;25:2559–2564 — ABS (RCT, 24 sedentary adults, 6 weeks)
- 7 abdominal exercises, 2 × 10, 5 days/week, diet held constant. "no significant effect of abdominal
  exercises on body weight, body fat percentage, android fat percentage, android fat, abdominal
  circumference, abdominal skinfold". Curl-up endurance improved (47 vs 32 reps).

### A2. Brobakken MF, et al. Physiol Rep 2023;11:e15853 (PMC10680576) — ABS (RCT, 16 overweight men, 10 weeks)
- Treadmill running plus 4 × 4 min of torso rotation and crunches at 30–40% 1RM, 4 days/week, vs
  energy-matched running only. Trunk fat fell 7% in the abdominal group and did not change in the
  running-only group; total fat loss was similar. The authors conclude "spot reduction exists in adult
  males"; they also call the wider evidence "equivocal". Small sample; overweight men; high weekly volume.

## Which exercises work the rectus abdominis hardest?

### A3. Oliva-Lozano JM, Muyor JM. Int J Environ Res Public Health 2020;17:4306 (PMC7345922) — FT (systematic review, 67 EMG studies)
- Values as % of maximal voluntary contraction (MVIC), rectus abdominis (RA):
  - Static curl-up, hands behind neck: 81%; arms crossed: 68%.
  - V-sits ≈ 80%; front plank ≈ 78% in one study, 32–41% in others (wide spread between studies).
  - Roll-out plank on a stable surface: upper RA 100%, lower RA 74%; suspended versions higher.
  - Double leg raise 43%; ordinary curl-up 37–51%.
  - Free weights: the Bulgarian split squat and back squat at a 6-rep max showed the highest RA activity
    of any exercise in the review (one study).
- Heterogeneous methods; no meta-analysis possible. EMG shows activity, not growth (see exercises X1).

### A4. Clark KM, et al. J Strength Cond Res 2003;17:475–483 — ABS (EMG pilot, 8 adults)
- No significant difference between upper and lower RA in any of 6 exercises; curl-ups gave higher RA
  activity than the reverse curl-up, leg lowering and ball roll-out.

### A5. Mandroukas A, et al. J Funct Morphol Kinesiol 2022;7:67 (PMC9505236) — ABS (EMG, 35 men, 11 exercises)
- Leg movements from lying favoured the RA over the external oblique; dynamic sit-ups favoured the
  external oblique.

## Does core training help running?

### A6. Hung KC, et al. PLoS One 2019;14:e0213158 (PMC6407754) — ABS (RCT, 21 male college athletes, 8 weeks)
- 3 core sessions/week: plank endurance up, and oxygen cost lower at the fastest treadmill stage.

### A7. Sato K, Mokha M. J Strength Cond Res 2009;23:133–140 — ABS (RCT, 20 completers, 6 weeks)
- Core training group ran 5000 m faster; no change in ground reaction forces or leg stability.

## Not found
- No trial on the sets/reps that grow the abdominal muscles in healthy adults.
- No meta-analysis settling spot reduction (A1 and A2 disagree).
- Nothing on diet; out of scope for this log.
